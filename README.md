# Enchantment Overhaul

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11 that completely rethinks the enchanting system.

Vanilla enchanting mixes stat boosts with real magical effects in a single system, then forces players to waste enchantment slots on mandatory statistical taxes. This mod separates everything into three distinct systems:

- **Smithing Table** handles all stat boosts (damage, protection, durability, speed) through templates found in structures, paid with materials and XP.
- **Material Properties** give armor innate specialized defenses (fire, projectiles, explosions, magic) based on what it's made of — no enchantment needed.
- **Enchanting Table** is reserved exclusively for magical effects, unlocked through exploration, paid with Lapis + Reagents + XP, and limited by a slot system tied to item material.

XP becomes the universal progression currency: enchanting, smithing, repairing via Mending — everything competes for the same resource. Farms stay relevant throughout the entire game.

The mod adds 12 new enchantments, reworks the anvil into a pure maintenance station, and introduces a crafted bait system to replace the Lure enchantment.
