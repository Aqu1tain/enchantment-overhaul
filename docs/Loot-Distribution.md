## Pre-Enchanted Equipment in Chests

Equipment found in structure chests has a small chance of spawning with one random enchantment at level 1. The chance scales with the slot count of the item:

| Slot Count | Chance |
|---|---|
| 3 (Leather, Wood, Stone, Copper) | 9% |
| 4 (Iron, Chainmail) | 12% |
| 5 (Diamond, Netherite) | 15% |
| 6 (Gold) | 18% |

Formula: slot count x 3%. Only enchantments compatible with the item are rolled. Curses, Mending, and disabled enchantments are excluded.
