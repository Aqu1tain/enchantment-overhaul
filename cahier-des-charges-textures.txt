========================================================================
  TEXTURE SPECIFICATION
  Enchantment Overhaul (Fabric Mod for Minecraft 1.21.11)
  v2 — March 2026
========================================================================

CONTEXT
-------
This mod overhauls Minecraft's enchantment system. Stat-based
enchantments (Sharpness, Protection, Unbreaking, Efficiency) are
replaced by 4 smithing templates. The rest (magic enchantments) goes
through a new enchanting table UI.

Project wiki: https://github.com/Aqu1tain/enchantment-overhaul/wiki

Current state: all code is functional, but no custom textures exist
yet. Templates show as pink/black squares in-game.


========================================================================
  PART 1 — TEMPLATE ITEM TEXTURES
========================================================================

There are 4 "smithing template" items to create. They work exactly
like vanilla smithing templates (Netherite Upgrade, armor trims).

CONSTRAINT: the textures must be visually consistent with vanilla
Minecraft 1.21 smithing templates. The player must immediately
recognize them as smithing templates. Use the Netherite Upgrade
Smithing Template as the primary reference — these are functional
upgrades, not cosmetic trims.

Beyond that constraint, full creative freedom on patterns and colors.

Format: 16x16 pixels, PNG, Minecraft palette (no anti-aliasing).

Files to produce:
  1. honing_template.png
  2. warding_template.png
  3. tempering_template.png
  4. grinding_template.png

Destination path:
  src/main/resources/assets/enchantment-overhaul/textures/item/

What each template does:

  HONING
  - Increases weapon damage (swords, bows, crossbows, spears, maces)

  WARDING
  - Reduces incoming damage on armor

  TEMPERING
  - Increases durability of any equipment

  GRINDING
  - Increases mining speed of tools (pickaxes, shovels, hoes, axes)

Each template must have a distinct visual identity, recognizable at
a glance even at 16x16.


========================================================================
  PART 2 — ARMOR UPGRADE OVERLAYS
========================================================================

When a player applies a template to armor, a visual overlay appears
on top of the base texture. The system works like vanilla armor trims:
a transparent layer composited over the armor texture. Overlays must
remain compatible with cosmetic trims — a player can have a vanilla
trim AND one or two upgrade overlays on the same piece.

Only 2 templates apply to armor:
  - WARDING (protection)
  - TEMPERING (durability)

They are NOT mutually exclusive. Armor can have both at the same time.
Both overlays must be designed to work individually AND stacked
together without becoming unreadable.

Visual intent:

  WARDING: the armor should feel sturdy, reinforced, heavy. It looks
  like it can take a beating.

  TEMPERING: the armor should feel refined, treated, enduring. The
  metal has been perfected. It won't break.

  Both together: a masterwork piece. Both tough and lasting.

The overlays can modify the armor silhouette, but in a restrained
way — the base armor must remain recognizable.

The overlays must work for all armor materials in the game (leather,
chainmail, iron, gold, copper, diamond, netherite). They are delivered
in grayscale — the renderer tints them to match the armor material
automatically, like vanilla armor trims.

Format: 64x32, PNG with transparency, GRAYSCALE only.
Brightness defines the tint mapping (light gray = highlight,
dark gray = shadow).

Files to produce (2 patterns x 2 layers = 4 files):

  Layer 1 (helmet + chestplate + boots):
    warding_armor_layer_1.png
    tempering_armor_layer_1.png

  Layer 2 (leggings):
    warding_armor_layer_2.png
    tempering_armor_layer_2.png


========================================================================
  GENERAL GUIDELINES
========================================================================

Everything should feel like a Mojang-added feature. If a player
doesn't know the mod is installed, the textures should look like
they shipped with the base game.
